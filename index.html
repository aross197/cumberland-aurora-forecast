Optimal Viewing Tips for Cumberland County

  • Kp needed: Typically Kp 5+ for a chance on the northern horizon; Kp 6–7 for a better display. Strong storms (Kp 7+) can bring vivid lights farther south.
  • Best hours: 10 pm – 2 am local (EDT/EST). Astronomical darkness is key.
  • Moon: New moon or thin crescent is ideal. Bright moonlight washes out faint aurora.
  • Location: Escape Portland light pollution — head north/northwest (e.g. near Freeport, Yarmouth, or inland toward Gray/Windham). Dark northern horizon essential.
  • Weather: Clear skies required. Check local cloud cover separately.
  • Camera tip: Use a tripod, high ISO, 5–15 s exposure — cameras often see more than the eye.
